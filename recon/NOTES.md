# Live Naano notes

Signed up 11 Sep 2026 as a brand.

- Site: https://naano.com
- Register is `/register` then `/register?role=saas` for brands. Creator vs brand cards first.
- Join with LinkedIn, Google or email. Email asks first name, last name, business email, password, how did you hear.
- 6 digit email code. Mine was 161101. Welcome email arrived a few minutes later.
- After login you land on `/brand`, not a marketing dashboard. Left rail is icons: Overview, Creators, Campaigns, Collaborations, Results, Messages, Billing.
- Overview for a new account is mostly zeros plus a to-do list (wallet, book a call, find creators). There is a Barkan assistant overlay.
- New campaign has three paths: Naano team, Create with AI, start from an existing link.
- Wallet is a prepaid balance, not a subscription, on the free plan.
- Pricing on the marketing site: Self-Serve €0/mo, Managed €700/mo. Posts from €20.
- Logos on the homepage: ringover, attio, La Growth Machine, lemlist, ChatSEO, Abyssale, BlogSEO.

I rebuilt the same loop with seeded campaign data so reviewers can click through a workspace that already looks alive, instead of an empty onboarding.
